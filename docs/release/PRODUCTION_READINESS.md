## 1. Compliance & Legal Review (DPDP Act 2023 & Consumer Protection)
- **Brand Consistency**: MIRSTOERA branding verified across all public documents, meta descriptions, and page titles.
- **Privacy Policy**: Clear notice outlining data collection, retention period, purpose limitation, and user rights in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.
- **Terms & Conditions**: Explicit pricing transparency, cancellation terms, advance notice requirements, and clear notice that booking is confirmed only upon concierge consultation.
- **Zero Real PII Leakage**: Synthetic accounts used during all automated audits. Real customer telephone numbers are never displayed publicly.

## 2. SEO & Discoverability
- **Sitemap**: Canonical `sitemap.xml` mapping all public routes with appropriate change frequency.
- **Robots.txt**: Permits search spiders to index public pages while disallowing `/admin/` and `/api/` endpoints.
- **OpenGraph & Schema.org**: JSON-LD Structured Data for `LocalBusiness` and `Event` curation embedded in head templates.
